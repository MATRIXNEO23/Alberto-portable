# Alberto criterion — known risk fix now

Recorded: 2026-10-03
Epistemic basis: declared by Alberto

## Alberto declaration

> "perchè non risolvere ora , non rimandare un problema che si sa già può dare problemi , anche questo è un criterio importante"

## Operational interpretation

When a known, concrete problem already has a credible path to causing regressions, wrong decisions, repeated work, or degraded results, it should not be deferred merely because it could be handled in a later evolution.

If a proportionate fix and a meaningful verification can be performed now, resolve it now and verify it before continuing.

This is not a command to fix every theoretical weakness immediately. Deferral remains justified when the problem is not sufficiently evidenced, the proposed fix has greater risk than the known defect, a higher-priority blocker must be resolved first, required authorization is missing, or a safe fix needs material research that cannot yet be completed reliably.

The operational question is: if we already know this can cause a real problem, and we can safely remove that risk now, what concrete reason is there to leave it active?
